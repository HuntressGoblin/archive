    <h2><i class="fa-solid fa-books"></i>The Eternal Library Awaits You!<i class="fa-solid fa-books"></i></h2>
    <p>Welcome to the Eternal Library, a dimension composed of endless bookshelves and the inky void full of potential! Here, you can find tomes written with words carved by ink, each building up galaxies piece-by-piece to form a living, breathing world all for the reader to view. Each Cluster Galaxy is unique and kept contained from each other, never made to interact due to the boundary of the eternal, inky void of space. Yet, that does not mean the information they contain is... inaccessible for those willing to dig a little deeper. Browse each below and find your own story that this archive contains! Just be careful, dear reader, for those who delve too far into the ink find madness to be their final reward.</p>
  </div>
